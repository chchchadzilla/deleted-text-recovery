# Code of Conduct

People arrive at this project on a bad day. Many of them are not technical. Treat everyone the way you would want to be treated an hour after losing something you worked hard on.

## What we expect

- Be patient and plain-spoken. No question is too basic here.
- Assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Protect privacy. Never ask anyone to share recovered text or real browser files, and never post someone else's.
- Give credit, and accept correction gracefully.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or mocking someone for what they do not know.
- Sexual, discriminatory or threatening language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Using this tool, or encouraging others to use it, to read another person's data without their permission.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainers through
<https://github.com/chchchadzilla/deleted-text-recovery/security/advisories/new>.
Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

This applies to the repository, its Discussions and Issues, and anywhere someone is representing the project.
